I'm Naman Aggarwal, a Member of Technical Staff at Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, working on platform engineering for the security organization. I enjoy digging into distributed systems: how their components interact, what happens when they fail, and the trade-offs that make them reliable.

My interests span databases, networking, operating systems, and security. I learn by building small implementations, debugging real behaviour, and reading the ideas behind the systems we use.

I studied Computer Science and Engineering at IIIT Delhi. During my time there, I worked with the Network Security Lab on an e-voting system and served as a teaching assistant for Advanced Programming.

This site brings together projects I've built and ideas I write about. Reading is my personal bookshelf, with brief notes on books, papers, and articles. Longer thoughts and essays live in Writing.
